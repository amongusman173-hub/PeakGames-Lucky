📋 Changelog - What's New

♠️ Poker Buy-In & Raise Slider + Card Reveal

  • 💵 Texas Hold'em now uses a buy-in system: the amount you bring to the table is deducted up front, and whatever you never risk is refunded when the hand ends — verified: $300 buy-in, raise $50, fold → $250 refunded
  • 🎚️ Raise now opens a betting slider instead of doubling automatically — pick any amount from the minimum up to ALL IN (your whole remaining stack), with Confirm / Cancel
  • 🃏 Bots now show their cards at showdown — their hole cards flip face-up with each bot's hand name and a WINS / BEATEN / LOST badge (verified: Pair · BEATEN, Straight · BEATEN)
  • 🤫 Bots only fold when you've actually bet against them — check/calling sees the board free, and a big raise pressures weak hands out (all-in bluff verified: $75 win on a $50 raise at 1.5x)
  • 🐛 Fixed the showdown winner detection — the winning bot is now correctly marked (it compared hands by object reference before, so nobody was ever flagged as the winner)

🎲 Grid View Redesign, Poker Betting & Fixes

  • 🖱️ Clicking the logo now opens a redesigned game grid: all 30 games, live search with synonyms ("poker" finds Texas Hold'em), and a gold ▶ PLAYING badge on the game you're in
  • 🎚️ Fixed the dice slider — it had zero height (the flex layout overrode it), so dragging was broken; the gold knob now tracks your drag smoothly
  • 🤖 Fixed the Texas Hold'em bot selector — the controls select and the table row shared an ID, so the first hand wiped the bot options (you couldn't pick bots); options now survive deals
  • 💵 Poker now has real betting rounds: after every street (pre-flop / flop / turn / river) choose Raise (double your stake, up to 4×) · Call · Fold, with your stake tracked live
  • 🤫 Bluffing works: weak bots fold under raise pressure — get them all to fold and you win your stake × multiplier without a showdown (verified: everyone folded → 1.2x win)
  • 🐛 Fixed a bug where the poker showdown could re-run if you kept clicking Call — bets are now locked the instant the hand settles
  • 🃏 Solitaire: face-up cards now fan out wider (46px steps) so you can read the whole stack at a glance, and you can finally place Kings on empty columns — click the empty column to drop a King there

♠️ Texas Hold'em Poker, Fixed Roulette Wheel & QOL Update

  • ♠️ NEW GAME: Texas Hold'em Poker — bet on your hand against 1-5 AI bots; every extra bot adds +0.1x to your win multiplier (1 bot = 1.1x, 5 bots = 1.5x)
  • 🎲 Bots' cards stay face-down the whole hand — you only see your cards, the community board, and your opponent's best hand name at showdown; split pots return your bet
  • 🎡 Fixed roulette: the wheel's colors were one segment out of phase with the numbers (red landing on black) and the pointer stopped on segment edges — segments are now generated from the real number order and the pointer lands dead-center on the result
  • 🎡 Roulette numbers are now drawn on the wheel rim; Lucky Wheel spins get custom ticking + whoosh synth sounds
  • 🎚️ Dice got a fully custom slider (drag the gold knob, tick marks at 2/50/98) and Loaded Dice is nerfed — a guaranteed win now pays a capped 1.5x instead of the full multiplier (49x at target 98)
  • 📊 Session Statistics page redesigned: gold gradient header, hover-lift stat cards with glow, framed graph
  • 🪙 Coin flip polish: motion blur while spinning, landing bounce + brightness flash
  • 🃏 Solitaire fixes: the stock now actually deals when clicked (it was gated on the wrong flag), hidden cards no longer lift on hover, and all its sounds are custom synth — no more slot/spin sounds
  • 🏆 New achievement: Poker Champion (first Hold'em win)
